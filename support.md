# Support — 5 an Hour

**5 an Hour** limits the apps you choose to 5 minutes of use per hour. When your 5 minutes are up, those apps lock until the next hour begins — enforced by Apple's Screen Time technology, automatically, all day.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I set it up?
1. Open 5 an Hour and approve the Screen Time permission.
2. Tap **Change tracked apps** and pick the apps that eat your time.
3. Turn on the **5-minute hourly limit**.

That's it. The rule runs by itself from then on.

### Why can't I turn the limit off right now?
By design. While your apps are blocked, the off switch is locked — the moment of craving is exactly when you'd cheat. The block always lifts automatically at the top of the next hour, and the switch unlocks with it.

### An app is still blocked after the hour changed. What do I do?
Open 5 an Hour once — it re-checks and repairs the schedule whenever it launches. This can happen in rare cases if the phone was off across an hour boundary.

### Why doesn't my usage number match the Screen Time app exactly?
5 an Hour counts only the apps you track, across your devices, and shows a per-device breakdown. Compare against Settings → Screen Time for the same device and the same apps, and the numbers should line up.

### How does the streak work?
A day counts toward your streak if the limit stayed on the entire day. Turning the limit off — even briefly — breaks the streak for that day.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Go to **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 5 an Hour** to change plans or cancel. Cancelling during the free trial means you're never charged; you keep access until the trial or paid period ends.

### I resubscribed on a new phone and it's asking me to pay again
Tap **Restore Purchases** on the unlock screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

### I have an offer code. Where do I enter it?
Tap the limit toggle to open the unlock screen, then tap **Redeem Code**. Codes can also be redeemed in the App Store app under your profile → *Redeem Gift Card or Code*.

### What data do you collect?
None. No account, no server, no analytics. Your app choices and usage never leave your device — see the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
