# Privacy Policy — 5 an Hour

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

5 an Hour ("the app") is a screen-time self-control tool for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: **your data never leaves your device.**

## What the app does

5 an Hour uses Apple's Screen Time technology (Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks) to let you limit apps you choose to 5 minutes of use per hour. After 5 minutes, the chosen apps are blocked until the next hour begins.

## Data we collect

**None.** 5 an Hour has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network code. We cannot see your app usage, your selected apps, or anything else about you.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following, only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- The apps you choose to limit (as opaque tokens provided by iOS — the app itself cannot read their names)
- Timestamps of when blocks started and ended, kept for 60 days to show your weekly statistics
- Your limit on/off setting and streak history

Apple's Screen Time system provides usage durations for display inside the app. This usage data is processed on-device by iOS and is never transmitted anywhere by 5 an Hour.

## Data sharing

We share no data with anyone, because we have none. If you use the share button to post your weekly stats image, you choose where that image goes.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app. All stored data is removed with it.

## Children's privacy

5 an Hour uses individual Screen Time authorization and is intended for users managing their own device usage.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the change noted in the App Store release notes.
